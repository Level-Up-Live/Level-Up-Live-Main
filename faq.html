Frequently asked questions

What is Level Up Live?
Level Up Live is immersive airsoft-style play on an indoor range. Real-time scoring and gear that feels real—no controllers. You move, you aim, you compete.
Do I need to bring my own gear?
We provide gear, briefing, and a safety run-through. You can bring your own clear glasses if you have them. At most locations you may bring your own airsoft gun if it's in a bag or case, unloaded, and 360 fps (The CUBE does not allow personal guns). All BBs must be purchased on-site.
Is it safe?
Yes. We use non-lethal airsoft, and our target wall is engineered to break BBs on impact to prevent ricochets. Eye protection and safety rules are required.
Do I need to book in advance?
Walk-ins are welcome at most locations. For groups or events, we recommend booking ahead.
Can I host a party or event?
Yes. We offer private arenas and packages for birthdays, corporate events, and groups. Use the location finder to choose a venue and get in touch.
