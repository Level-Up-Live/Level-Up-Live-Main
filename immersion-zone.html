live in the game.

Step into a fully interactive shooting environment with reactive targets, instant hit feedback, and live digital scoring. The Immersion Zone is a 6-lane system—every round feels more like a premium game arena than a traditional lane setup.

What It Is

The Immersion Zone is not a standard range. It is a 6-lane reactive gameplay environment where targets respond, score updates instantly, and each session is designed for replayability. Multiple lanes run in parallel for throughput—players can jump in casually or run competitive sessions that challenge speed, accuracy, and decision-making.
