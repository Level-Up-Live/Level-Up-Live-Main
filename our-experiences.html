Our Experiences

Three distinct ways to play. Pick the format that matches your group, skill level, and vibe.

Immersion Zone

High-energy interactive gameplay with real-time scoring and fast replay loops.

  • Best For: Groups, first-time players, and competitive social events that want immediate action.
  • What You Get: Reactive targets, instant hit feedback, and challenge modes designed for repeat play.

Explore Immersion Zone

Action Zone

Mission-style rounds and pressure-driven gameplay that reward strategy and pace.

  • Best For: Players who want higher-intensity game scenarios with movement and tactical decisions.
  • What You Get: Objective-based modes, team coordination, and competitive challenges that push performance.

Explore Action Zone

Live Fire Zone

Precision-focused live fire with sensor-equipped steel targets and measurable results.

  • Best For: Shooters who want authentic live fire progression, clear metrics, and leaderboard performance.
  • What You Get: Sensor-verified scoring, structured drills, and competitive runs built around precision and control.
