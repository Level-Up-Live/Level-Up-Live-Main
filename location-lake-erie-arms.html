This facility features 16 lanes of interactive shooting using Action Target's multi-scenario target retrievers giving shooters endless training possibilities. This is next level training!

8 shooting suites

Our TopShot ClayHouse offers a one-of-a-kind experience, much like Topgolf, offering games modes like Trap, Skeet, Sporting Clays, and 5-Stand. Enjoy a round of clays with up to 4 shooters per suite!
