Partner hub

Experience zones, program notes, and partner pricing for approved partners.

Immersion Zone

Reactive gameplay built for replayability—ideal for entertainment venues and mixed-traffic locations.

Guests step into a fully interactive environment with reactive targets, instant hit feedback, and live digital scoring. Sessions work for casual drop-in play and competitive runs without feeling like a traditional static lane.

Partner fit: Strong where you want high throughput, photo-worthy energy, and repeat visits from groups and families.

View Immersion Zone

Action Zone

Movement-heavy stages and head-to-head energy for guests who want to run, compete, and reset fast.

Targets on stands support dynamic layouts, shoot-offs, and run-and-gun style courses. It is built for competitive energy and replayable sessions that keep groups engaged.

Partner fit: Great when your audience skews younger or event-heavy and you have floor space for movement-forward gameplay.

View Action Zone
