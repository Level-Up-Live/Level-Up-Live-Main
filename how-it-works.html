How it works

1

Choose a venue

Pick the Level Up Live location you want to play at. You can book ahead for the best time slots, or walk in if you’re flexible.

2

Sign up for a player profile

You can do this before you arrive by going to player sign up. Your profile helps us get you started faster when you check in, so you can spend more time gaming.

3

Gear up

Our safety host will walk you through the basics: how to operate the airsoft gun and how to use the player tablets at your station. You’ll get set up and ready to play before your first round.

The CUBE: No personal guns. Other locations: You may bring your own. Guns must be in a bag or case, unloaded, and 360 fps. All BBs must be purchased on-site.

4

Get some food

Grab bites and beverages while you get warmed up. Eat before your matches or order as you play so you stay energized.

5

Choose your game

Pick your game, get your first round started, and jump right into the action. Complete missions, track your score, and aim for the top of the leaderboard.

6

Come back again

Bring friends, try new game modes, and keep leveling up. When you come back, you’ll be ready faster and spend even more time playing.
