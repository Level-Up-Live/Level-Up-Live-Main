About Us

Who We Are

Welcome to Level Up Live. We have reimagined target shooting as a modern sports entertainment experience powered by technology, live gameplay, and competition. Our venues are designed to feel energetic, social, and beginner-friendly while still offering real challenges for experienced shooters.

We use airsoft equipment and smart target systems to create a realistic but safe experience. Players can step in, learn quickly, and start scoring in minutes. With live feedback, interactive game modes, and leaderboard-style play, each round feels active, competitive, and rewarding.

Our mission is to make shooting sports more accessible to more people, with a focus on skill, precision, and fun. Whether you are coming with friends, family, coworkers, or a larger event group, the experience is built around participation, teamwork, and positive competition.

Behind the scenes, our patented technology includes hit-detecting targets, immersive projection, player interaction screens, game effects, and connected scoring systems that support drills, game modes, achievements, and progression. This gives us the flexibility to create experiences for casual guests, serious competitors, and professional users. Our team is made up of engineers, gaming enthusiasts, competition shooters, and AI software specialists working together to keep pushing the experience forward.

We are building Level Up Live as a long-term global brand, with expansion across major U.S. markets and future international growth. Our goal is simple: create unforgettable experiences that help people improve skills, enjoy healthy competition, and have an amazing time every time they step into the game.
