About Us

Who We Are

Level Up Live is building the next generation of immersive shooting entertainment. We combine advanced mechatronics, game design, and real-world interaction to create a high-energy, social experience that is accessible to first-time players and competitive regulars alike.

Our mission is to blend technology and gameplay into unforgettable venues where players can train, compete, and level up together in safe, guided environments.
